Employment

We act for employers and senior employees. We prefer to solve problems early, and we're ready to go to tribunal when that's the right answer.

What we do

  • Contracts and staff handbooks
  • Disciplinary and grievance processes
  • Redundancy and restructuring
  • Settlement agreements
  • Tribunal claims
  • Restrictive covenants

Common questions

My employer has offered me a settlement agreement. What now?

You must get independent legal advice for it to be binding. Your employer usually contributes to the cost, and we can often advise within 48 hours.

Can you write our staff handbook?

Yes. We draft handbooks on a fixed fee and offer an annual update service as employment law changes.

Common questions

How long does a commercial lease take?

Typically four to eight weeks, depending on the landlord and any works. We give you a realistic timetable at the start.

Can you act for both landlord and tenant?

No. To avoid a conflict of interest we act for one side only.
